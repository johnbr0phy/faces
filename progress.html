This run

Six seeds against the Mannay plates. The critic scored five pieces (ink, hair/hats, head, body, name). Refs still won 5 / 5. No piece flipped. v2 / v2b stay on the page as the obsolete stick-figure baseline.

  • Ink — still short butt-capped fBm polylines on a flat cream (hash-noise grainPass, opaque polygon fills) rather than a dry nib that skips and pools inside real paper tooth. Cause: dude.js inkLine (~w butt segments, 0.32px fBm) plus grainPass.
  • Hair / hats — still fail to wrap the 3D skull as dark filled masses that replace the crown silhouette. They read as empty scalps, temple stickers, toupees, or thimble-hats perched on the oval.
  • Head — every seed is still the same 7%-jittered ellipsoid with traits stamped on, not a lumpy seed-specific oval owned by one heavy dry brow-to-tip nose.
  • Body — every body is still the same trapezoid chassis with parallel-offset ribbon tubes for limbs, so it reads as a robot kit rather than a few naïve ink contours. drawBody builds a 10-point torso then ribbon() offsets a 3-point spine; clothes.kind only adds ticks.
  • Name — jittered all-caps typeface (forced uppercase geometric glyphs, even tracking, same cheek-height stamp) rather than a dry-ink note. The leftover Georgia italic “another dude” button is typeset chrome that does not share that ink.
